Olaf Ronneberger, Philipp Fischer, Thomas Brox

Introduced an encoder-decoder CNN architecture with skip connections for precise pixel-wise segmentation. Designed specifically for biomedical images with limited training data.

Results:
Achieved state-of-the-art segmentation accuracy. Became the standard architecture for medical image segmentation tasks.
3. 3D U-Net: Learning Dense Volumetric Segmentation from Sparse Annotation

Authors:
Özgün Çiçek, Ahmed Abdulkadir, Soeren S. Lienkamp, Thomas Brox, Olaf Ronneberger

Extended U-Net to 3D by using 3D convolutions for volumetric data (.nii). Enabled learning from sparse annotations and improved spatial feature extraction across depth.

Results:
Improved segmentation accuracy on 3D medical datasets. Demonstrated strong performance in volumetric tumor detection tasks.

Authors:
Fausto Milletari, Nassir Navab, Seyed-Ahmad Ahmadi

5. Attention U-Net: Learning Where to Look for the Pancreas

Results:

6. EfficientNet: Rethinking Model Scaling for Convolutional Neural Networks

Mingxing Tan, Quoc V. Le

Introduced compound scaling method to uniformly scale depth, width, and resolution of CNNs. Designed efficient architecture for high performance with fewer parameters.
